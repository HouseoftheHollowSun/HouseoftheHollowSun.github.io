Our Chronicle

A brief history of our glorious history and rise to the top

The Genesis of the Void


In the early cycles, the Landsraad stood as a closed gate to those walking alone. Seeking influence, Ma’an Til stood apart from the existing political machines, rejecting the call of established banners. Out of this solitary ambition, the House of the Hollow Sun was forged—not as a gathering of the weak, but as a crucible for those who refuse to serve. It began as a whisper in the dunes, a singular will carving out territory where others saw only risk.

The Twin Scourge

The ascent accelerated when Izumi stepped from the shifting sands to join the cause. For months, the two operated as a silent, lethal collective—a death squad that acted with singular precision. They did not merely compete for territory; they dismantled opposition. By maintaining an unshakable grip on the Landsraad rankings, the duo proved that sheer tenacity and combat efficiency could override the numerical superiority of larger, bloated factions.

The Expanding Shadow

The transition from a lethal pair to a commanding power began with the induction of Exe, an act that signaled a new era of dominance. The House shed its isolationist shell, rapidly absorbing those who possessed the grit to thrive under our banner. We did not grow by chance, but by design. Today, we stand as the preeminent Harkonnen force on the Nova server, a testament to what can be achieved when raw power is unified under a single, Hollow Sun.
